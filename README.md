# University Projects

**Projects Completed During My Master's at École Polytechnique and ENSAE**

This repository showcases a collection of projects from various courses undertaken during my Master's degree. Most projects are implemented in Python and include both code and a detailed write-up of the research conducted. Each project reflects the course content and the skills acquired throughout my studies.

Feel free to explore the projects, and don’t hesitate to reach out if you have any questions or would like to discuss any of the work in more detail.

## Projects Included

* **Python for Data Science — Movie Recommendation System**
  *Collaborative/content-based recommenders with evaluation and model selection.*

* **Optimisation — Steepest Gradient Descent Analysis**
  *Convergence properties, step-size strategies, and conditioning effects (with experiments).*

* **Machine Learning — ML Techniques for Asymmetrical Binary Data**
  *Class imbalance & asymmetric loss: calibrated metrics, resampling, and cost-sensitive models.*

* **Markov Processes — Modelling Jumping Electrons**
  *Continuous-time Markov chains for electron jumps; generator, stationary law, simulation.*

* **Time Series — Analysis of French Defense Industry Production**
  *Index decomposition, seasonality, ARIMA/structural models, and shock attribution.*

* **Dynamic Models with Latent Variables — Stationarity & Ergodicity of MS-PCM Models**
  *Study of “Stationarity and Ergodicity of Markov Switching Positive Conditional Mean Models”.*

* **Machine Learning in Finance — Deep Optimal Stopping**
  *Implementation/replication of deep stopping methods; payoff design and training stability.*

* **Algorithmic Trading — Mean-Field Game of Market Making vs Strategic Traders**
  *Exploration of MFG formulation, equilibrium characterization, and numerical schemes.*

* **Portfolio Management & Risk — Common Volatility Drivers in a Multi-Asset Universe**
  *Empirical identification of COVOL factors (Campos-Martins & Engle) and risk implications.*

* See each project’s `README.md` for specific instructions.
